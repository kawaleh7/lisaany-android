Privacy Policy — Lisaany

Last updated: September 17, 2026

Lisaany ("we", "our") is an Arabic-learning app and website. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

Information we collect

  • Account information. If you create an account, we store your email address and a securely hashed password through our authentication provider (Supabase).
  • Learning progress. Lessons completed, quiz results, streaks, XP, review schedules, and your learning goal, so your progress is saved and synced across devices.
  • Device and usage data. Basic technical information (app version, device type, crash reports) to keep the app working and to deliver content updates.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, or microphone audio. If you use the app without an account, progress is stored only on your device.

How we use it

  • To provide and improve lessons, review scheduling, and your learning path.
  • To sign you in and keep your progress.
  • To send account emails (confirmation, password reset). We do not send marketing email without your consent.

Sharing

We do not sell your data. We share it only with service providers that run the app on our behalf: Supabase (accounts and progress), Cloudflare (hosting and audio delivery), and Capgo (delivering content updates to the app). Each is bound by its own privacy and security terms.

Payments

Premium purchases made inside the Android app are processed by Google Play. We never see your card details.

Children

Lisaany is intended for learners aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Data retention and deletion

We keep your account data while your account is active. You can delete your account and all associated progress at any time by emailing us at the address below; we complete deletion within 30 days.

Security

Data is transmitted over HTTPS and stored with industry-standard protections by our providers.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by contacting us.
